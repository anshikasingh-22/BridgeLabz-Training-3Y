Welcome to Lucknow

Lucknow is the capital city of Uttar Pradesh. It is famous for its rich history, beautiful architecture, traditional culture and delicious food.

Famous Places

Lucknow has many beautiful and historical places such as Bara Imambara, Chota Imambara, Rumi Darwaza and British Residency.

Bara Imambara

Bara Imambara is one of the most famous historical monuments in Lucknow. It is known for its unique architecture and Bhul Bhulaiya.

Rumi Darwaza

Rumi Darwaza is a beautiful historical gateway and one of the most recognizable landmarks of Lucknow.

Famous Food

Lucknow is famous for its delicious Awadhi cuisine. Popular foods include Galouti Kebab, Tunday Kebab, Biryani, Chaat and Lucknowi Basket Chaat.
